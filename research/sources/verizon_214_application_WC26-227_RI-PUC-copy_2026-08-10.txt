II.      GRANTING THE APPLICATION ADVANCES THE BUILD AMERICA
         AGENDA.

         The Commission’s Build America Agenda is making it easier and faster for providers to

retire copper and modernize U.S. communications infrastructure. 7 Approval of this Application

will advance the Build America Agenda and serve the public interest in multiple ways.

         First, approval will drive investment toward networks of the future and reduce the need

to devote resources to infrastructure of the past. Investment is essential to the Build America

Agenda, which aims to “free up billions of dollars for new networks, instead of forcing providers

to keep investing in old ones.” 8 Keeping resources locked up in old copper-based POTS

networks will thwart unleashing that investment.

“the expansion of [ ] modern networks, and the benefits they afford, have been hindered by the

need for carriers to divert important resources to the maintenance of aging and deteriorating

legacy networks that deliver outdated services to an ever-decreasing number of subscribers.” 9
