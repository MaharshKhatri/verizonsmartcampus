 Description of the Planned Network Change(s):

 Verizon intends to retire the copper facilities at all remaining locations in the sixty-two (62) wire
 centers listed above. The copper facilities being retired support services that depend on obsolete
 time-division multiplexing and also use SS7 signaling. Verizon will discontinue services offered
 over these copper facilities pursuant to FCC rules before retiring the facilities. Locations affected
 by this copper retirement will have access to another wireline option, including VoIP service
 from Verizon or a cable company, or fixed or mobile wireless service from multiple providers
 including Verizon Wireless.

 Description of Reasonably Foreseeable Impact(s) of the Planned Change(s): After the
 retirement of the copper facilities, interconnecting carriers will need to make alternative
 arrangements for services formerly obtained via these copper facilities. Verizon offers multiple
 modern products and services available to replace services formerly provided over these copper
 facilities. To help ensure an orderly transition, Verizon may require customers and
interconnecting entities to migrate services off of copper facilities well before the copper
retirement implementation date.
