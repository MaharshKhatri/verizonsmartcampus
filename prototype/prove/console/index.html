How long does a broken HELP phone stay broken?

120 cars for a year. Mean days from failure to repair, by how failures get found. Rates are assumptions (below); the pilot replaces them.

Silent faults (speaker, mic, unanswered calls) are the only failures a line monitor misses. Scaling them from 0.1× to 3× keeps PROVE's reduction between and , because nothing else finds them for months.

Why two tones, not one loopback?

Hands-free phones cancel their own echo or mute the mic while the far end talks, so a single tone played out and listened for back can fail on a healthy phone. Results are from a model; the bench test on a real elevator phone (Gate B) decides.

Evidence ledger

Every test is appended to a SHA-256 chain. Editing any past result breaks every hash after it. Your browser checks the chain itself. (Voluntary record, not a compliance certificate.)
