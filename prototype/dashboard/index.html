    Today's AI and Circadian share one planner, one set of radios, one SLA guard and one margin learner; only the forecast differs. On ordinary days they tie (Circadian blends in traffic history when the calendar says the day is normal). On event days today's practice must choose between congestion (no event mode) and wasted energy (event mode); Circadian avoids both.

    Event days: today's practice vs Circadian

    Today's practice = traffic-history AI plus a manual "event mode" that switches energy saving off on venue cells from 2 h before to 4 h after a ticketed event.

    Faults in this scenario

    The twin knows which radios are asleep on purpose, so it only flags real faults. It diagnoses each fault from its symptoms, runs a remote fix, and sends a truck with the right part only for hardware failures.
